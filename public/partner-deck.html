PRELIMINARY PARTNER DECK01 / 05
The idea

Real challenges.
Responsible prototypes.

Partners bring bounded, non-confidential challenges and practical expertise. Student teams bring engineering, curiosity and collaborative effort.

For student builders

Applied learning, mentorship, teamwork and a demonstrable prototype.

For partners

Challenge-led engagement, optional workshops and fair access to meet emerging talent.

Seven focused tracks

AI; Defence & Aerospace; Cybersecurity; Space & GeoAI; GovTech; Climate & AgriTech; Open Innovation.

Responsible by design

Public, synthetic or authorized data. Independent judging and written scopes.
