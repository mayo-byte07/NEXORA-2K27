The NEXORA model

Real problems.
Responsible
prototypes.

A structured, transparent collaboration platform: companies and institutions bring real, bounded challenges; student teams build demonstrable technology prototypes.

Partners can share expertise, offer tools, mentor teams and meet emerging talent—within a written scope, fair process and clear safeguards.

01 / For students

Learn by building

Work on practical challenges, learn from mentors and make a prototype with a team.

02 / For partners

Bring your expertise

Share a bounded challenge, tools, workshops, mentorship or in-kind support.

03 / For everyone

Meet emerging talent

Connect through opt-in, fair processes and a transparent showcase.
